About Us

History SantriCyber

SantriCyber is an organization or community, it is focused on providing Islamic religious education as well as knowledge and skills related to cybersecurity for students of Islamic boarding schools in Indonesia. The organization was founded in 2007 with the goal of creating students who understand the development of the computer world and make it easier for students to spread Islamic teachings and make it more accepted by the friends through the internet. The organization is intended to make the students more updated and adaptable with the development of technology, also to help them to spread the religious teachings easily. The organization aims to make the students become someone who is useful for the society by providing them with the knowledge about cybersecurity and Islamic teachings. The organization has several domains to make it easier for students to access the community and its mission is to provide a platform for students to share their knowledge and skills in both Islamic education and cybersecurity, and to help fill the gap in knowledge for students who lack knowledge about cybersecurity. more..

Why Choose SantriCyber?

  • 01 What makes SantriCyber different from other platforms?

    SantriCyber is a unique platform that integrates Islamic values with modern technology. We provide a space where both santri and non-santri can learn about cybersecurity, programming, and digital literacy while staying true to ethical principles.

  • 02 How does SantriCyber help me improve my knowledge?

    We offer a dedicated forum where you can ask questions, share knowledge, and discuss topics with other members. Additionally, we plan to provide online courses, tutorials, and community events to enhance learning experiences.

  • 03 Is SantriCyber a paid service?

    No, SantriCyber is free to join and use. Our goal is to empower the community by providing accessible education in both Islamic knowledge and digital skills.

  • 04 Who can join SantriCyber?

    SantriCyber is open to everyone, regardless of background or expertise. Whether you are a student, developer, entrepreneur, or just someone interested in learning, you are welcome to be part of our community.

  • 05 How can I contribute to SantriCyber?

    You can contribute by sharing knowledge, writing blog posts, participating in discussions, and even helping with platform development. If you have expertise in a particular field, we encourage you to become a mentor to help others grow.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What services does Santri Cyber provide?

    We offer a variety of services, including Islamic and technology discussion forums, app development tutorials, graphic design resources, and cybersecurity guidance.

  • Is Santri Cyber only for santri (Islamic students)?

    No, anyone interested in learning about Islam and technology is welcome to join our community.

  • How can I access the Santri Cyber forum?

    You can access our forum at santricyber.dev. Registration is free and open to everyone.

  • Are there any training sessions or webinars available?

    We plan to host webinars and online courses soon. Stay updated by following our website and social media channels.

  • How can I contribute to Santri Cyber?

    You can contribute by sharing knowledge in the forum, writing articles for our blog, or assisting in platform development.

  • Is there a membership fee to join Santri Cyber?

    No, joining Santri Cyber is completely free. We believe in open access to knowledge and community-driven learning.

  • Can I promote my own projects or businesses in the forum?

  • How do I report inappropriate content or behavior in the forum?

    You can report any violations of our guidelines through the forum’s reporting system or by contacting our moderators directly. We take community safety and respect seriously.

  • Can I collaborate with Santri Cyber for educational or community projects?

    Absolutely! We welcome collaborations with individuals and organizations that share our vision of promoting Islamic knowledge and technology. Feel free to reach out to discuss potential partnerships.
